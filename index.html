How to play

  • Follow and repel

    Show a spread palm to attract ducks, or a fist to repel them. With two hands, each controls its own flock after six ducks join.

  • Grab Space

    Pinch your thumb and index finger, or hold Space. Nearby ducks stay protected until you let go or the hold times out.

  • Attack slam X

    Hold up your index finger and pinky with the other fingers and thumb tucked, or press X. Hits enemies near the hand.

  • Defense slam C

    Start with two fists apart and bring them close, or press C. Pushes ducks away from danger and puts out flames on them.

  • Punch Z

    Press Z or tap Punch to hit an enemy at close range.

Guide stray ducklings back to the flock for points. Survive as the zones and enemy attacks grow harder.

Action buttons also work in webcam mode. Opening this guide pauses the run.
